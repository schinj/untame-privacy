Untame Yourself Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Untame Yourself does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data. Everything the app knows stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores on your device

To enforce the limits you set, Untame Yourself keeps the following on your device only:

  • The app groups you create, their daily time budgets, and lock settings.
  • A per-day log of how much of each budget you've used.
  • Your answers to the optional setup questions (for example, your goals and when you tend to overuse apps).
  • Lock state, kept in the device Keychain so limits can't be bypassed by editing app data.

This data is saved in the app's private storage and in an App Group shared only with the app's own Screen Time extension. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Screen Time

The app uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework to apply the limits you choose. The apps and websites you pick are represented by opaque tokens from Apple. The app can't see their names or your browsing and usage history, and that information never leaves Apple's frameworks on your device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local notifications (for example, when you've used half a budget). They're generated on your device; no push service is involved.

No accounts, tracking, or analytics

There are no accounts, no servers, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting services, and no third-party SDKs. The app makes no network requests.

Deleting your data

Deleting Untame Yourself removes your groups, budget log, and setup answers from your device. Lock state is the exception: it's kept in the device Keychain, which iOS doesn't clear when an app is deleted, so a lock that's still active applies again if you reinstall. Like everything else, it stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. You can also revoke Screen Time access at any time in Settings.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes
