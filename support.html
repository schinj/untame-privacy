Common questions

How do I set a limit?

Tap New Group, pick the apps or categories you want to limit, set a daily budget, and choose how long to lock it. You can also add a downtime schedule that blocks the group completely during set hours.

What happens when I use up my budget?

Apple's Screen Time blocks the group's apps for the rest of the day. The budget resets the next day.

Why can't I unlock a group early?

That's the point of the lock. You set it when you're thinking clearly so it still holds when you're tempted. The lock ends on the date shown in the group.

What can I change while a group is locked?

Only changes that make it stricter: a smaller budget, more apps, a longer downtime, or a later end date. Raising the budget, removing apps, or shortening the lock is refused until the lock ends.

I reinstalled Untame and a lock came back. Why?

Locks are stored in the iPhone's Keychain, which iOS keeps when an app is deleted. Deleting the app removes your groups but not an active lock. To reconnect it, tap Reconnect it and pick the same apps as before. Apple doesn't tell Untame which apps you picked, so it can't fill them in for you.

I'm not getting the halfway notification.

Check that notifications are allowed for Untame Yourself in Settings → Notifications.

Can I turn Untame off?

Yes. You can revoke Untame's Screen Time access in Settings at any time, and its limits stop applying. Untame can't prevent that. It's your phone.

Can Untame see which apps I use?

No. Apple gives Untame opaque tokens for the apps you pick, not their names or your usage history. See the privacy policy.
